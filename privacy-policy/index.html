Privacy Policy for FridgeGrub

Last updated: August 15, 2026

FridgeGrub ("the app") is developed by Pancakeworks. This policy explains what data the app collects, stores, and shares.

Summary

FridgeGrub does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. It has no network access, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

FridgeGrub stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android's standard app-private storage:

  • The ingredients you've added to your fridge list, including quantities, units, and expiration dates you enter
  • Which recipes you've marked as favorites
  • Your chosen app mode (Checklist or Quantity)

None of this data ever leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer, to any server, or to any third party. The app has no network permission and makes no network requests. Recipe and ingredient data used for search and matching is bundled with the app itself and never updated or queried over the internet.

Data collection

FridgeGrub does not collect any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, or analytics of any kind. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and nothing tying app data to your identity.

Data sharing

FridgeGrub does not share any data with anyone, because it does not transmit any data in the first place.

Data deletion

All app data is stored locally and is removed automatically when you uninstall the app. You can also clear it at any time via Android's Settings > Apps > FridgeGrub > Storage > Clear Data.

Children's privacy

FridgeGrub does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
